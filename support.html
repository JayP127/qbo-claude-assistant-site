Support

The Benemetry QuickBooks assistant ("Claude assistant") is an internal tool of Benemetry Ltd. Support is for Benemetry staff who use it.

Contact

Email jay@benemetry.com with the subject line "QuickBooks assistant". Please include what you asked Claude, the error message if any, the date and time, and whether you were using the Claude Desktop chat or a Code session. We answer on UK business days.

Security

If you believe the tool's credentials have been exposed, or you see activity in QuickBooks that the tool should not have been able to perform, write to the same address with "Security" in the subject line and disconnect the tool straight away. Benemetry can also regenerate the app's secret in the Intuit developer portal.

Not covered here

  • QuickBooks Online itself: contact Intuit's support from within QuickBooks.
  • The Claude applications: contact Anthropic's support.
  • Members of the public: this tool is not available outside Benemetry. For anything else about Benemetry, write to the address above.
