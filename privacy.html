Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Who we are

Benemetry Ltd ("Benemetry", "we") is the data controller for the processing described on this page. Our company details are in the footer. Questions and requests go to jay@benemetry.com.

What this policy covers

It covers the Benemetry QuickBooks assistant, registered with Intuit as the app "Claude assistant": Benemetry's internal connection between its own QuickBooks Online company and Claude, the AI assistant made by Anthropic. It does not cover benemetry.com, which has its own policy, or QuickBooks Online itself, which is governed by Intuit's terms and privacy statement.

What the tool does

When an authorised member of Benemetry staff asks Claude a question about the company's books, a small program running on that person's computer fetches the relevant records from Benemetry's QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's API and hands them to the Claude application, which uses them to answer. Where creating records is enabled, the tool shows each new record to the staff member and sends it to QuickBooks only after they approve it. The tool cannot change or delete existing records: those functions are switched off in the software and blocked in the Claude application.

What data is involved

Benemetry's own accounting records in QuickBooks Online: the chart of accounts, customers and suppliers with their contact details, invoices, bills, payments, bank and expense transactions, VAT codes, financial reports, and the names of employees where they appear in those records. Most of this is business data. Some of it is personal data about Benemetry's customers, suppliers, staff and contacts: names, business contact details and the amounts invoiced or paid.

Why we process it

To keep Benemetry's books, prepare financial reports and answer the questions of the people who run the company. Under the UK GDPR our lawful bases are our legitimate interest in running our own accounts efficiently and our legal obligation to keep accounting records.

Where the data goes

  • Intuit. The tool reads from, and where enabled writes to, Benemetry's QuickBooks Online company through Intuit's API, under Intuit's developer terms.
  • Anthropic. Records the tool fetches become part of the staff member's conversation with Claude and are processed by Anthropic's Claude service to compose the answer. Anthropic processes them under the terms of Benemetry's Anthropic account, whose settings do not allow Anthropic to use the data to train its models.
  • No one else. We do not sell this data, do not share it with advertisers and do not share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Where it is stored and for how long

  • Credentials. The tool stores the access credentials Intuit issues to it in a file on the staff member's computer that only that person's user account can read. Revoking the tool's access in QuickBooks makes them useless, and they are deleted when the tool is disconnected (see Disconnect).
  • QuickBooks data. The tool itself keeps no copy of QuickBooks data. Reports that Claude writes from the data are saved in a private Benemetry repository and kept as part of the company's accounting records, which UK company law requires us to keep for at least six years.
  • Conversations. Anthropic's retention of conversation data follows the terms of Benemetry's account.
  • Location. The staff member's computer is in the United Kingdom. Anthropic may process data outside the United Kingdom; the terms of Benemetry's account govern those transfers.

How we protect it

  • All traffic to Intuit and to Anthropic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Credentials never leave the staff member's computer and are stored in a file readable only by that person's user account.
  • Only named Benemetry staff who are administrators of the QuickBooks company may connect the tool.
  • The tool cannot change or delete records, and every record it creates needs a person's approval first.

Your rights

If you are a Benemetry customer, supplier, employee or contact, you have rights under the UK GDPR over your personal data: to ask what we hold, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and in some cases to receive it in a portable form. Write to jay@benemetry.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
